## Hands-On Workshop: Building Intelligent Financial Workflows with Agentic AI and MATLAB
Overview
Join MathWorks to learn ways to use Agentic AI.

This hands-on workshop explores how financial institutions can leverage MATLAB and the Agentic AI Toolkit to build intelligent workflows for research, analysis, and reporting.

Through guided exercises and live demonstrations, participants will learn how AI agents can interact with data, execute MATLAB analyses, implement multi-step workflows, and accelerate economist/researcher/developer productivity while maintaining transparency and control.

Highlights
During our workshop, each Participant will access MATLAB, an AI Agent, and learn about:

- Connecting an AI Agent to MATLAB
- Improving agentic AI behavior through skills
- Ways to share work and collaborate with others, including through apps

Who Should Attend
This workshop can benefit anyone interested in how to get started with AI and use it to solve practical financial or economic modeling problems.
